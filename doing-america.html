📍 Luray Caverns on Maps
🦇
Luray Caverns Must-Do📅 Book Ahead
Largest cavern in eastern US — cathedral rooms, crystal pools, 140-ft columns. The Great Stalacpipe Organ (world's largest instrument) plays every ~5 min. Ticket includes Car Museum, Heritage Village and Toy Town Junction. ~1.5 hrs underground.
Book at luraycaverns.com. Add the Rope Adventure Park at checkout for Tom and Ido.
🧗
Rope Adventure Park @ Caverns 🔥 Teens: Tom, Ido, Alma, Karni
High and low ropes courses at the Caverns grounds. Two difficulty levels — high course for 12–15 year olds (Tom, Ido, Alma, Karni), low course great for Lulu (7) with an adult. Zip lines, rope bridges, aerial obstacles. Buy alongside the cavern ticket.
🌿
Garden Maze @ Luray Caverns All Ages
1-acre hedge maze — 8-ft walls, hidden tunnels, clues to find, climbing observation tower. "Speed run and race it with a friend 😀" — perfect teen competition 😀.
🥩Lunch Options
Triple Crown BBQ ⭐ Best in Valley
1 mile from Luray Caverns. Smoked ribs, pulled pork, legendary corn muffins and baked beans. Opens mid-April — perfect timing.
"Excellent meats smoked to perfection. The baked beans tasted like they cooked IN the smoker. Bought enough for a leftover dinner." — Google

🪁En Route Stop — Kitty Hawk
📍 Kitty Hawk Kites on Maps
🦅
Kitty Hawk Kites 🔥 Teens
World's largest hang gliding school since 1974. 30+ activities: hang gliding lessons, kite flying, kayaking, paddleboards. Tom, Ido, Alma and Karni can do tandem hang gliding with an instructor — no experience needed.
1–2 hr stop. Hang gliding intro ~$100/person — one of the most memorable things on the OBX. Book online at kittyhawk.com.
✈️
Wright Brothers National Memorial Free
The exact spot where the first powered flight happened in 1903. Quick 10-min stop right next to Kitty Hawk Kites. Surprisingly moving for everyone — All the teens will feel the scale of what happened here.
🌅Afternoon — Arrival Nags Head
📍 Jockey's Ridge State Park
🏜️
Jockey's Ridge Sand Dunes 🔥 TeensFree
Tallest active sand dune system on the East Coast — 80–100 ft. Hike up, run down, fly a kite from the ridge. Views of both the ocean and the sound. The teens will race each other to the top. Spectacular at sunset (5–6pm).
🏖️
First OBX Beach Walk All AgesFree
April OBX: wide, uncrowded sand. Water is still cool (low 60s) — no swimming, but great for walking the shoreline, frisbee, or just breathing salt air after the drive.

Basnight's Lone Cedar Café Soundside

📍 Corolla, NC on Maps
🐴
Corolla Wild Horse Tour ⭐ Trip Highlight📅 Book Now
2-hour open-air safari truck through the 4x4-only beaches north of Corolla. ~100 Colonial Spanish Mustangs descended from horses brought by explorers in the 1500s. Guides share history, spot dolphins and deer. Safe for all ages including Lulu (7)!
Book: Wild Horse Adventure Tours (wildhorsetour.com) or Bob's (corollawildhorsetours.com). ~$50–60/person. Book the earliest slot possible — 8–9am for best sightings.

Jennette's Pier Fishing 🔥 Teens

🎡Evening — Ocean City Boardwalk
📍 Ocean City Boardwalk
🏎️
Jolly Roger Speedworld — Go-Karts 🔥 Teens
Largest go-kart facility on the East Coast — 10 tracks including the 5-story Cyclone! Tom (15) + Alma (15) drive solo on big tracks. Ido (12) + Karni (12) on family tracks. Also has the Giant Ferris Wheel with ocean views and the Slingshot thrill ride.
30th Street location. Budget ~$20–30/person for a few rides. One of the best teen activities in OC.
🕹️
Marty's Playland + Fun City Arcade 🔥 Teens
Two best arcades on the OC boardwalk. Marty's is the 2024 Best of Ocean City winner — skee-ball, claw machines, ticket games. Fun City right on Caroline St. Tickets never expire — save them if you come back.
"I've been going since the '60s and it's still my go-to boardwalk arcade. Adult kids love it." — TripAdvisor
⛳
Mini Golf — Nick's or Old Pro Group Tournament
Dinosaurs, pirate ships, jungle temples, indoor glowing courses — OC has mini golf on every corner. Perfect family-group competition. Nick's also has a new ropes course for the teens. ~$10/person.
👻
Trimper's Haunted House + Rides 🔥 Teens
America's oldest single-family operated amusement park (since 1893). Award-winning haunted house — great for 12–15 year olds. Tidal Wave boomerang coaster, classic carousel, and more at the inlet end of the boardwalk.
🍟
Boardwalk Food Tour OC Classics
Thrasher's French Fries (vinegar only — since 1929!), Fisher's Popcorn (caramel), Kohr Brothers soft-serve, Dumser's ice cream. Everyone picks their own while walking the boards.
Thrasher's is vinegar-only — no ketchup. This is non-negotiable OC tradition. The debate WILL happen. Vinegar wins.
🍽️Dinner Options
Coastal Salt ⭐ Oceanfront
Stylish new American restaurant right on the boardwalk. Ocean views, indoor & outdoor seating. Crab cakes with Chesapeake aioli, seared duck breast, craft cocktails. Best sit-down on the OC boardwalk.
"Steak Frites, Smoked shrimp dip, Coffee Creme Brulée — excellent as always. Wonderful view of the water on the boardwalk." — Google
